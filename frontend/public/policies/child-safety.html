Important Safety Information

Corded window coverings can pose a strangulation hazard to young children. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends cordless or motorized window treatments in homes with children.

Safety Tips for All Window Treatments

Even with safe window treatments, follow these best practices:

  • Keep furniture away: Move cribs, beds, and furniture away from windows
  • Inspect regularly: Check for any loose cords or damaged components
  • Install correctly: Follow mounting instructions to ensure secure installation
  • Supervise children: Always supervise young children near windows
  • Replace old blinds: Consider replacing corded blinds manufactured before 2018

Rooms to Prioritize

We especially recommend cordless or motorized options for:

  • Children's bedrooms and nurseries
  • Playrooms and family rooms
  • Living rooms with low windows
  • Any room where children spend time

Upgrading Your Current Window Treatments

If you have corded window treatments, consider these options:
