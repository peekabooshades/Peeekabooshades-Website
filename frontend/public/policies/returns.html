Measurement Errors

Since all products are custom-made to your specifications, we cannot accept returns for measurement errors. We strongly recommend double-checking your measurements before ordering. Need help? Check our measuring guide or contact us for assistance.

Exchanges

Need a different size, color, or style? We offer exchanges within 30 days:

  • Contact us to initiate an exchange
  • Return the original item in its original packaging
  • We'll manufacture and ship your new item
  • Price difference (if any) will be charged or refunded

Refund Process

Once we receive your return:

  • Items are inspected within 2-3 business days
  • Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method
  • Credit card refunds appear within 5-7 business days
  • PayPal refunds are typically faster (1-2 business days)

Restocking Fee

A 15% restocking fee may apply to returns that are not due to manufacturing defects or our error. This helps cover the cost of custom manufacturing. Defective items and our errors are always refunded in full.

Returns FAQ

How do I start a return?
Contact our customer service team via email at returns@peekabooshades.com or call us. We'll provide an RMA number and return shipping instructions.
Who pays for return shipping?
For defective items or our errors, we provide a prepaid return label. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
Can I return a motorized shade?
Yes, motorized shades follow our standard return policy. However, they must be uninstalled and returned with all components including the motor and remote.
What if my order arrived damaged?
Please take photos of the damage and packaging, then contact us within 48 hours. We'll arrange a replacement at no additional cost.
