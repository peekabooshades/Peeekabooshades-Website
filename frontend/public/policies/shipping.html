Shipping Options & Delivery Times

All Peekaboo Shades products are custom-made to your specifications. Please allow manufacturing time before shipping.

Shipping Method Manufacturing Time Transit Time Cost
Standard Shipping 5-7 business days 5-7 business days Free over $199 / $14.95
Express Manufacturing 3-4 business days 5-7 business days +$29.95
Rush Delivery 3-4 business days 2-3 business days +$49.95

Important Note

Manufacturing and transit times are estimates. Custom products require careful craftsmanship to ensure quality. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by:

  • Logging into your account and viewing order history
  • Using the tracking number provided in your shipping email
  • Contacting our customer service team

Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to all 50 United States. International shipping is available for select countries. Contact us for international shipping quotes.

Texas Local Delivery

Located in Texas? We offer special local delivery options for customers in Dallas, Houston, Austin, San Antonio, and Fort Worth metropolitan areas. Contact us for local delivery pricing and availability.

Packaging & Handling

Your custom window treatments are carefully packaged to ensure they arrive in perfect condition:

  • Each shade is individually wrapped in protective material
  • Hardware and mounting brackets are packaged separately
  • Installation instructions included with every order
  • Oversized orders may ship in multiple packages

Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with your delivery, please contact us immediately:

  • Damaged packages: Document damage with photos before opening
  • Missing items: Check all boxes - items may be packaged separately
  • Wrong items: Contact us within 48 hours of delivery

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my order?
Standard orders typically arrive within 10-14 business days (5-7 days manufacturing + 5-7 days shipping). Express options are available for faster delivery.
Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
Address changes can be made before manufacturing begins. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to update your shipping address.
Do you ship to P.O. boxes?
Due to the size of our products, we cannot ship to P.O. boxes. Please provide a physical street address for delivery.
What if I'm not home during delivery?
The carrier will typically make multiple delivery attempts or leave the package in a safe location. You can also provide delivery instructions during checkout.
