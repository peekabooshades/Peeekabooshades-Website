What's Covered

Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use:

Component Coverage Period What's Included
Fabric & Material 5 Years Fading, discoloration, fraying, delamination
Lift Mechanisms 5 Years Chain, cord, and spring mechanisms
Hardware 5 Years Brackets, headrails, bottom rails
Motorized Systems Lifetime Motor, remote control, smart hub
Solar Panels 3 Years Charging functionality, connections

What's Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear from regular use
  • Damage from improper installation
  • Damage from accidents, misuse, or abuse
  • Damage from cleaning with unapproved products
  • Damage from extreme environmental conditions
  • Products that have been modified or altered
  • Products used in commercial applications (different warranty terms apply)

Pro Tip: Extend Your Warranty

Proper care extends the life of your shades. Follow our care instructions, avoid harsh chemicals, and use motorized shades within their rated cycles for best results. Regular dusting with a soft cloth keeps fabrics looking fresh.

How to Make a Warranty Claim

If you believe your product has a warranty-covered defect:

  • Step 1: Contact our customer service team with your order number
  • Step 2: Provide photos showing the defect
  • Step 3: Our team will review and respond within 2-3 business days
  • Step 4: If approved, we'll arrange repair or replacement

Keep Your Receipt

Your proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims. We recommend keeping your order confirmation email and saving a copy of your invoice.

Transferability

Our warranty is transferable to subsequent homeowners. If you sell your home, the new owners can continue to enjoy warranty coverage for the remaining warranty period. Simply provide them with the original purchase documentation.

Warranty FAQ

When does my warranty start?
Your warranty begins on the date of delivery, not the purchase date. This ensures you have full coverage from the moment you receive your products.
What if my shade stops working after the warranty period?
We offer out-of-warranty repair services at reasonable rates. Contact us for a repair quote. Many issues can be resolved with replacement parts.
Does the warranty cover installation damage?
Damage caused during installation is not covered. We recommend following our installation guides carefully or hiring a professional installer.
Is there a commercial warranty?
Yes, we offer separate warranty terms for commercial applications. Contact our sales team for details on commercial warranty coverage and pricing.
