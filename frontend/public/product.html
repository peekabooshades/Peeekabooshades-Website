Motorized Roller Shades — 65% Light Control, Linen Texture

Bring soft natural light, privacy and convenient motorized operation to your windows with custom Roller Shades from PeekabooShades. The linen-inspired fabric provides approximately 65% light control, helping reduce glare and create a comfortable indoor atmosphere without making the room completely dark. Its subtle woven texture gives windows a warm, refined appearance that works well with modern, transitional and traditional interiors.

Designed for indoor windows and doors, these custom motorized Roller Shades can be operated by remote control and may support mobile-app, scheduling and compatible smart-home controls depending on the selected motor and accessories.

Why You'll Love These Roller Shades

The approximately 65% light-control fabric reduces harsh sunlight while allowing filtered daylight into the room — practical for spaces where you want privacy and glare reduction without a complete blackout effect.

Recommended Rooms

These light-filtering Roller Shades are well suited for:

  • Living rooms, dining rooms and kitchens
  • Home offices and family rooms
  • Hallways and media spaces where complete blackout is not required
  • Interior doors with compatible dimensions
Not intended to provide complete darkness. Choose a 100% blackout Roller Shade for bedrooms, nurseries and home theaters requiring maximum room darkening.

Motorized Control Options

Remote Control

Operate one shade or organize multiple compatible shades into remote-control channels. A six-channel remote is part of the standard product package.

Mobile-App Control

Compatible motor configurations may allow customers to open, close or position shades through a mobile application.

Voice Control

Depending on the selected motor and smart-home accessories, voice operation may be available through platforms such as Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant or Apple Home.

Automated Scheduling

Compatible smart controls may allow shades to open or close at selected times, including morning and evening schedules.

Smart-home functionality depends on the exact motor, bridge, application and platform selected. Compatibility must be confirmed before ordering.

Mounting Options

Inside Mount

Installs within the window frame for a clean, built-in appearance. Before selecting: measure inside width at top/middle/bottom and inside height at left/center/right, confirm sufficient mounting depth, and check for handles, locks or obstructions.

Small light gaps may remain along the sides because the fabric is narrower than the full headrail assembly.

Outside Mount

Installs above the window opening or on surrounding trim/wall — preferable when inside depth is limited, extra privacy/coverage is desired, the frame is uneven, or hardware obstructs an inside mount. Adding overlap beyond the opening helps reduce visible side light.

Standard Package Contents

  • One custom motorized Roller Shade
  • One six-channel remote control
  • Mounting brackets
  • Installation screws and hardware
  • Charging cable
  • User manual
Package contents can vary by selected motor or accessory configuration. Confirm the final order summary before purchase.

Installation

Supports inside- or outside-mount installation. The manufacturer states installation generally requires a drill, ruler or measuring tape, and pencil. Estimated time is approximately 15–20 minutes for a typical installation; actual time depends on window, wall material, shade size and hardware.

  • Confirm the mounting location and mark bracket positions
  • Check that brackets are level, then secure with suitable fasteners
  • Attach the shade or cassette to the brackets
  • Charge and test the motor, then pair the remote control
  • Program upper and lower limits when required
  • Test the complete opening and closing movement

Care & Cleaning

Use a soft microfiber cloth, feather duster or low-suction vacuum attachment to remove surface dust. For small marks: use a lightly dampened cloth, blot gently instead of rubbing, avoid soaking the fabric, and avoid abrasive cleaners, bleach and harsh chemicals. Do not bend or crease the roller fabric.

Always disconnect charging accessories before cleaning around the motor or electrical components.
