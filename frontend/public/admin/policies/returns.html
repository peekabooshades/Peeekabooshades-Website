Return Policy Overview

At Peekaboo Shades, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Because our window treatments are custom-made to your specifications, we have specific guidelines for returns and exchanges.

Return Window

Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery for most products.

Custom Products

Due to the custom nature of our products, returns are only accepted for:

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Incorrect measurements (if we measured)
  • Damage during shipping
  • Wrong product delivered

Products made to customer-provided measurements are not eligible for return due to sizing issues.
