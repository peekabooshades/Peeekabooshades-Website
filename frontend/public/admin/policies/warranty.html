Warranty Coverage

Peekaboo Shades stands behind the quality of our products with comprehensive warranty coverage.

Limited Lifetime Warranty

All window treatments include warranty coverage against:

  • Manufacturing defects in materials
  • Defects in workmanship
  • Premature fading (5 years)
  • Mechanism failures under normal use

Motor Warranty

Motorized products include a 5-year motor warranty covering:

  • Motor failure under normal use
  • Remote control defects
  • Battery issues (rechargeable motors)

What's Not Covered

  • Damage from improper installation
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage from accidents or misuse
  • Color variations from fabric dye lots
