Getting Accurate Measurements

Proper measurements are crucial for a perfect fit. Follow these step-by-step instructions to measure your windows correctly.

Our custom window treatments are made to your exact specifications, so accurate measurements ensure:

  • Perfect fit for your windows
  • Proper light control and privacy
  • Professional appearance
  • No returns due to sizing issues

Tip: Always use a metal tape measure for the most accurate results. Cloth tape measures can stretch over time.
