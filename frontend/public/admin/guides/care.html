Care & Maintenance Guides

Care Instructions by Product

🪟 Roller Shades

Dust regularly with soft cloth or vacuum with brush attachment. Spot clean with mild soap solution. Do not machine wash.

🦓 Zebra Blinds

Gently dust with microfiber cloth. For stains, use damp cloth with mild detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals.

⚡ Motorized Shades

Same fabric care as manual shades. Check battery levels monthly. Keep motor housing dry.

📜 Roman Shades

Professional cleaning recommended. Vacuum with low suction. Steam clean for deep cleaning.

General Care Tips

  • ✓ Dust weekly to prevent buildup
  • ✓ Clean spills immediately
  • ✓ Test cleaning solutions on hidden area
  • ✓ Avoid abrasive cleaners
  • ✓ Keep shades fully raised when cleaning windows
  • ✓ Operate shades gently

Warranty Note

Improper cleaning or care may void warranty. Always follow manufacturer guidelines.
